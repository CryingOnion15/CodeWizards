# Getting Started

When creating a wand you will be passed a creator to make them under. A creator will have leaning in what panels, connections, numbers, and types they like to use. This will influence what is more likely to appear in a wand when it is generated, but doesn't have to be followed strictly. The idea is to all flavor so when a player make a selection of which creator they want a wand from they have a bit of a expectation of the what the wand being generated might look like or more likely to have.
